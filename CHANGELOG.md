# Changelog

## 0.1.16 Beta · refreshed October 31, 2026

- Episode notifications skip episodes you have already watched.
- Fixed Notify me for upcoming titles, including titles without an announced date.
- Going back from a title restores your browsing position and loaded results.
- Search suggests matching titles as you type.
- Sharper loading artwork keeps the poster separate from the background.
- Home does less work between updates, with paused artwork changes when it is out of view.

- Refreshed installer with StreamNyaa branding; the app version remains 0.1.16.
- Fixed duplicate Continue Watching cards when episodes share a batch torrent.
- The back arrow sits at the top left of the player and hides with its controls.
- Buffering and subtitle loading keep the video in view instead of returning to the startup artwork.
- Playback stays inside the StreamNyaa window.
- F12 toggles fullscreen throughout the app. Escape leaves fullscreen after closing any open player panel.
- Back to Watch returns to the episode page. Alt+Left works during playback too.
- Fixed opening local files whose Windows paths contain a special path prefix.

## 0.1.15 Beta · October 29, 2026

The first public Windows beta.

- Improved series timelines and related-installment results.
- Season labels use confirmed information; missing or conflicting numbers remain unknown.
- Incomplete timeline results retain available entries and offer a way to continue or retry.

Includes individual and batch source selection, episode-file playback, movie episode limits, Sub-timed recent releases, local watch history, and episode-completion controls.
