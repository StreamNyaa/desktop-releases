# Privacy

StreamNyaa is a networked desktop app. Installing it does not make torrent traffic anonymous.

## What leaves your device

- Browsing sends the requests needed to show titles, artwork, schedules, and source results. Online services can observe connection information such as your IP address.
- Torrent peers, trackers, and discovery services participate in finding and transferring the selected torrent. Your IP address and the torrent's info hash can be visible to them. Playing a stream still transfers data, and the torrent engine may upload pieces while participating.
- Checking a torrent or a batch's file list can contact peers before video appears. Inspecting a pack does not mean the whole pack has been downloaded.
- Signing in sends the information needed to authenticate your account and synchronize supported app data. Normal connection and security records may be kept when you use online features.
- When signed in, supported account data is synchronized. Watched/title tracking covers all tracked titles; detailed cloud resumes are limited to the 20 most recently played anime. Local full history remains on that device. An older evicted cloud resume cannot restore an entire local archive to a new PC.
- External trailers, links, and sign-in pages connect to their respective providers when used.

Do not treat a VPN as a guarantee that a torrent is lawful, correctly labeled, or safe. Use only media you have the right to access and share.

## What stays on the device

The app keeps local history, resume information, preferences, temporary playback data, download state, and diagnostics. Files you download remain on your PC in the selected destination. Guest browsing and playback still use the internet.

Local app files can contain account information and viewing activity. Do not share the app's data folder or login/session information. Anyone with sufficient access to your Windows account or files may be able to read local records. Protect your Windows account and backups accordingly.

## Backups and deletion

Use **Export personal backup** in Settings before testing or changing versions. Personal backups include history and preferences, but exclude account credentials and downloaded media. A backup may still reveal what you watched; do not post it in a bug report.

Clearing history and resetting watched/completed tracking are different operations. Review the confirmation shown by the app. Signing out or uninstalling is not a request to erase cloud account records, and one device cannot erase another device's local archive through normal synchronization.

## Diagnostics and reports

Before sharing diagnostics, remove emails, account IDs, access/refresh tokens, full personal paths, private tracker URLs/passkeys, IP addresses you do not intend to share, and viewing history. Prefer a small relevant excerpt. Use private reporting for security-sensitive material.
